TITLE: SYNTHETIC TEST TEXT — Ferry Lane (not a real interview)
NOTE FOR USE: Invented text used only to test retrieval. It must never be presented as a real memory.

## The ferry

Ferry Lane is an invented riverside lane used for software testing. In this test text, a wooden ferry carries people across the river every morning, and the ferryman rings a small bell before leaving the bank.

The fare in this test text is two coins. Children wait on the stone steps and count the boats passing downstream while the ferry loads baskets of vegetables.

## Lanterns

In the evenings of this test text, a lamplighter hangs paper lanterns along Ferry Lane and takes them down again at dawn. When the wind blows out a lantern, he lights it again from a burning incense stick.
